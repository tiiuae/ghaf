---
title: "NVIDIA Jetson Orin: BPMP Virtualization"
---

The Boot and Power Management Processor (BPMP) controls Jetson clocks, resets,
and power domains. A guest that owns an integrated platform device may therefore
need access to a restricted set of BPMP operations.

The BPMP proxy drivers, host services, device-tree builders, and per-device
allow-lists are maintained by [jetpack-nixos][jetpack]. Ghaf consumes those
interfaces for supported VM passthrough, but does not carry copies of the
drivers or platform policy.

Each guest receives a dedicated `/dev/bpmp-host-<vm>` endpoint. The selected
jetpack-nixos policy limits that endpoint to the clocks, resets, and power
domains required by the assigned devices. Do not enable the unrestricted
development policy in a production image.

GPU and display demonstration compositions are maintained in the
[Ghaf GPU partitioning examples repository][examples]. MGBE0-to-NetVM remains
part of the Ghaf Orin hardware composition because it is the board's network
device assignment.

[jetpack]: https://github.com/tiiuae/jetpack-nixos
[examples]: https://github.com/tiiuae/ghaf-gpu-partitioning-examples
